Observations contain the actual game image and motor feedback, not structured game state.

Controls: WASD moves, Space jumps, left_shift sneaks, left_ctrl sprints, E opens/closes inventory, 1-9 selects a hotbar slot, and Q drops an item. Relative mouse motion changes view: positive dx turns right and positive dy looks down. Left mouse mines/attacks while held; right mouse uses/places.

Opening a menu does not pause this server. Held inputs persist until released, including during model reasoning. A wait is real elapsed time, not a world pause. Each action batch permits 1-64 commands and at most 10000 milliseconds of total explicit waits.
