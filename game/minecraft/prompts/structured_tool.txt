Observations contain the fixed native projection: position, health, inventory, nearby entities and a radius-15 block cube without line-of-sight filtering. Unloaded cells are unknown, not air.

nearby_blocks.data_rle contains [palette_index, repeat_count] pairs encoding the complete flattened cube, x fastest, then y, then z, starting at minp. Expanding runs reconstructs every original cell. Inventory slots 1-9 are the hotbar.

Tool arguments and effects are defined by the provided tool schemas. A normal tool refusal is reported as success=false. Observations arrive automatically after each tool batch.

When exposed by the task, AttackTool submits one native melee attack at the current crosshair within ordinary survival reach. It does not aim, move, repeatedly attack or wait for the weapon cooldown. UseHeldItemTool holds ordinary use on the selected item for a bounded duration, then releases it; native item and hunger rules apply. Neither tool chooses equipment or a recovery policy for you.
